CareBridge privacy

Short version: your information stays on your phone. CareBridge has no accounts, sells nothing and tracks nothing.

What CareBridge keeps

Your preferred name, language, visits, ride circle (first names, free times and optional phone numbers) and reminders are saved in your phone's browser. "Erase everything" in Settings deletes them.

Hospital connection

When you connect a hospital, you sign in on the hospital's own MyChart page. CareBridge never sees your password. It can only read your appointments, visits, basic details (name, date of birth, record number, preferred language) and medicine list. It cannot change anything in your record. You can remove CareBridge's access in MyChart at any time.

AI

To understand a request or read an appointment letter, CareBridge sends that sentence, letter or photo to Claude (Anthropic) and keeps only the visit details it extracts. Photos are not stored.

Calls, texts and notifications

If you use them, a phone call transcript, ride-request texts and replies, and reminder times pass through CareBridge's server for up to 14 days so they can be delivered, then expire. Calls and texts only go to numbers you approved. CareBridge always says it is an AI when it calls.
