CareBridge terms of use

CareBridge is a free student project that helps older adults and their families organize doctor visits and rides.

  • Not medical advice. CareBridge does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call 911.
  • Check the details. Always confirm appointment dates and times with your clinic. AI can make mistakes; CareBridge asks you to check before saving anything.
  • Rides are between family and friends. CareBridge only passes messages; it does not provide transportation.
  • Use your own information. Only connect a hospital account that is yours, or one you have permission to help with.
  • No warranty. CareBridge is provided as is, as a demonstration project.
